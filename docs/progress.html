How a piece clears

  • Operability ≥ 80% drivable
  • Zero P0 defects open
  • at most two P1, each owned
  • every fix has a test that fails without it

The first gate asked a critic to pick us over Stripe with zero blocking issues. That is unsatisfiable for real software, so it printed "reference wins" 25 times running and told us nothing. It now measures Operability — can a human actually complete the job on screen — and Defect Debt, both of which move.

The invitation works: mint a link, set a password, land inside the workspace. Six status vocabularies became one, 223 lines left the modules for 67 in the kit, and four guards stop a seventh appearing. The build contract now describes how this repo is actually built rather than how it was built ten waves ago. Still out: the revenue and payments leftovers and the end-to-end suite.

  • Usage metering & prepaid credits

  • Invoicing, credit notes & tax

    In review 90% debt 6 3 rounds

    CriticThe invoice arithmetic is Stripe-grade, but usage billed a month late, cancelled subscriptions left prorations unbilled, and the overview added euros to dollars.

  • Payments, retries & dunning

    In review 96% debt 4 3 rounds

    CriticThe money never lies here, but the recovery policy was harsher and narrower than Stripe’s: a 3DS card written off in a millisecond, a refunded bill owed by nobody, and no card-stolen family of declines.

Wave P0 · Criticals & blast radius

5/5

Every fix is now followed by an agent whose job is not to confirm it, but to hunt the same mistake in the reverse operation, the sibling call and the undo — because twice now a fix has created its own mirror image.

  • Atomic job claim

    Cleared 100% debt 0 1 round

    Two concurrent drains could both run one renewal, billing a period twice. Now UPDATE … WHERE status = pending; 3 of 4 new tests fail on the old code.

  • Rollups excluded from record merge

    Cleared 100% debt 0 1 round

    Defining any max rollup broke dedupe permanently. The first regression test I wrote passed with and without the fix and proved nothing; the second one fails correctly.

  • Refunds, overpayment & presentment

    Cleared 100% debt 0 1 round

    Overpayment is a ledger credit, not a second charge; a direct debit is presented once and settles days later; an idempotency key is scoped to its key’s owner.

  • Untaxed invoices & MRR truncation

    Cleared 100% debt 0 1 round

    An invoice with no resolvable tax location is held in draft and refuses to finalize or pay; US jurisdictions stack; MRR aggregates over every subscription rather than the first page.

  • Privilege, tenancy & client resilience

    Cleared 100% debt 0 1 round

    The copilot carries no more authority than its caller; the time machine is per workspace and one drain cannot run another’s jobs; one failed fetch no longer blanks the app.

  • Invitations, privacy and the trail

    Cleared 100% debt 1 1 round

A booked follow-up creates a task a person can open, reassign or cancel. A note’s subject is its first sentence, not its whole body. Money in answer prose prints one way, with cents, everywhere.

  • One vocabulary, and a door that opens

The invitation is now handed over once like an API secret, an invited seat looks like one everywhere people are listed, and the other end of the link — which had no screen at all — is a page that sets a password and lands you inside. Six status vocabularies became the kit’s one; two loading figures and three failed-read panels became one each; four workarounds for kit bugs came out after each was proven redundant. 223 lines left the modules, 67 entered the kit, and four static guards stop a seventh vocabulary appearing.

  • The build contract

    Cleared 100% debt 0 1 round

    The only coordination between parallel builders had drifted far enough that every brief was contradicting it in writing — it still told builders to run a shared build that races whoever else is in the checkout. It now describes the isolated preview people actually use, states the two rules learned the hard way (a fix ships a test proven to fail without it; after a fix you hunt its mirror image), names what the kit owns so nobody writes a fourth status pill, and states the one-company-one-identity rule as a rule, since breaking it twice put a falsehood on screen both times.
